LBH - Experience That Creates Value

An Israeli investment group founded by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski.

Who we are

LBH is an Israeli investment group founded by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, built on a combination of senior executive experience, financial expertise and a proven ability to develop and transform businesses.

The group's partners have led major companies and corporations in Israel and abroad, across infrastructure, transportation, logistics, industry and international operations.

The group takes an active investment approach in which value creation does not end at closing. It continues through core business development, operational improvement, management systems, innovation, organic growth and mergers and acquisitions.

The Dan and Gadot investments demonstrate the group's ability to combine investment complexity with execution experience and to grow businesses at significant scale.

How LBH invests

LBH invests through several vehicles. The investments in Dan and Gadot were made through the Value LBH funds (also referred to as LBH Value), established in partnership with the investment house Value Base. As reported, the funds are managed by Value Base M&A together with Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski. In Dan, the LBH Infrastructure fund invested together with Value Base in 2019.

The investment in Hinoman, the grower of Mankai, was made in 2022 by an investor group led by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, together with Kibbutz Be'eri. The founders' prior leadership roles, such as heading Mekorot or Israel Railways, are not investments of the group.

The Investment Philosophy Behind LBH

We invest in companies, but first we know how to build them.

LBH draws on the executive and business experience of Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, who have themselves run companies and corporations in infrastructure, transportation, logistics and international operations: the national water company Mekorot, Israel Railways, Egged, Maman and Gadot. That experience helps identify where value sits in a business, understand its operational complexity and lead change and growth alongside management.

The examples vary: at Dan, improving the transportation business and separating it from real estate through to the sale of control; at Gadot, building an international logistics platform and taking part in the Haifa Port acquisition; and at Hinoman, acquiring a company in crisis and leading its recovery. The first two were made through the Value LBH funds, the third by an investor group led by the founders.

Questions and answers about LBH

Who founded LBH?+

LBH was founded by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski. Ben Hamo's roles have included CEO of Mekorot and Deputy and Acting Director General of the Government Companies Authority. Linchevski has served as CEO of Israel Railways, Egged Holdings and Maman Group, and since 2018 as CEO of Gadot Group.

What does LBH specialize in?+

LBH invests in operating companies with growth potential, mainly in infrastructure, transportation and logistics, and creates value through executive involvement: strengthening the core business, operational efficiency, innovation and add-on acquisitions. Its key investments are Dan, Gadot and Hinoman.

How is LBH related to Value LBH?+

Value LBH (also referred to as LBH Value) is the name of the investment funds through which the group invested in Dan and Gadot. The funds were established in partnership with the investment house Value Base and, as reported, are managed by Value Base M&A together with Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski.

Which investments has the group been involved in?+

Dan Transportation, which the group invested in from 2019 and sold control of in 2026; Dan Real Estate, separated from Dan and retained by Value LBH; Gadot, in which the funds invested in 2021 and which took part in the Haifa Port acquisition; and Hinoman, the grower of Mankai, acquired in 2022 by an investor group led by the founders.

What is LBH's Buy & Build strategy?+

Buy & Build means acquiring a stake in a company with an operating base and building it into a larger platform. Across the group's investments it has been applied along four axes: core business development, operational efficiency, innovation and business development, and add-on acquisitions. At Dan and Gadot it was accompanied by EBITDA growth, per group data.
