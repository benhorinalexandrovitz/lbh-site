About the group

Executive Experience. Investment-Driven Growth.

LBH was founded by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, two senior executives with decades of experience leading major businesses across infrastructure, transportation, logistics and investment.

The group believes that sustainable value creation begins with a deep understanding of the business itself: its people, assets, operations, markets and growth drivers.

Combining executive leadership, financial expertise and hands-on execution, LBH develops value creation strategies, builds business platforms and drives long-term growth.

BUY & BUILD

Value Creation Model

A Buy & Build model, demonstrated at Dan and Gadot, along four axes.

  1. 01

    Core Business Development

    Strengthening the existing business, expanding activity, upgrading capabilities, developing infrastructure and creating growth engines.

    At Dan: electrifying some 1,200 buses, about 600 charging points and light rail tender wins.

  2. 02

    Operational Excellence

    Procurement, organizational structure, workforce management, productivity and performance improvement.

    At Dan: non-performance rate cut from 9% to 1.5%.

  3. 03

    Innovation & Business Development

    Adopting technologies, launching new activities, developing products and entering new markets.

    At Gadot: a blending and tolling facility in Belgium with low-flash capabilities.

  4. 04

    Mergers & Acquisitions

    Add-on acquisitions, platform expansion and operational and commercial synergies.

    At Gadot: seven acquisitions from 2021 to 2026, including Haifa Port alongside Adani.
