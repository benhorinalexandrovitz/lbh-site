Dan Transportation - From a Legacy Operator to a Growing Infrastructure Platform

The Dan investment demonstrates LBH's ability to identify potential in an established company, lead operational and business change, develop growth engines and build a broad transportation and infrastructure platform.

The company and the investment

Dan is Israel's second-largest public transport company. In 2019 the investment house Value Base and the LBH Infrastructure fund, managed by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, agreed to acquire 40% of Dan from the members of the former cooperative, at a company valuation of about NIS 1.26 billion. The acquisition closed in early 2020, and a year later the holding was increased to 50% at the same valuation.

At the time of the investment, Dan relied mainly on operating bus routes, with a non-performance rate of about 9%. Fund representatives served on the board and, together with management, led a multi-year plan to strengthen the core business, improve efficiency and enter new infrastructure segments.

Separation strategy

As part of the value creation strategy, the Dan Group's transportation, infrastructure and real estate activities were separated, reflecting their different business characteristics, growth options and financing needs. Each business can then be realized along the path that suits it.

Investment Exit: From Value Creation Strategy to Realized Value

In February 2026 an agreement was signed to sell control of Dan Transportation to an investor group led by Dan CEO Ofir Karni, with Clal Insurance, Leumi Partners and Mizrahi-Tefahot Invest. As reported, the transaction reflected an effective company valuation of about NIS 2.8 billion for Dan Transportation, compared with about NIS 1.26 billion for the whole Dan Group at the 2019 entry.

The sale of Value LBH's holdings closed at the end of June 2026, after Competition Authority approval. In October 2026 the second stage was also completed, with the investor group buying additional shares from Dan's members and reaching a holding of about 57%.

Dan Real Estate: Creating Value by Separating Businesses

The Dan Group's real estate business was separated from the transportation company and remained with Value LBH after the exit. Dan Real Estate holds land, development projects and income-producing assets, and following the transportation exit, steps were taken to enhance it and to explore combining it into a broader real estate platform.

On August 26, 2026, Dunietz Elad reported signing a non-binding memorandum of understanding with Dan Real Estate for a reverse triangular merger. If a definitive agreement is signed and conditions are met, Dunietz Elad will hold all of Dan Real Estate's shares in exchange for issuing shares to Dan Real Estate's shareholders. As of late October 2026, no binding agreement had been signed and the transaction had not been completed.

Questions and answers: Dan

When did LBH invest in Dan?+

In 2019 Value Base and the LBH Infrastructure fund, managed by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, agreed to acquire 40% of Dan at a valuation of about NIS 1.26 billion. The acquisition closed in 2020 and the holding rose to 50% in 2021. In the group's data the two investments appear as Class A and Class B.

How was the transportation business improved?+

Along four axes: core business development, including light rail tender wins and electrifying some 1,200 buses; operational efficiency, such as a lean headquarters and cutting non-performance from 9% to 1.5%; innovation through a mobility-tech fund; and add-on acquisitions, including an operator in Portugal. Per group data, Dan standalone EBITDA grew from NIS 171 million to NIS 547 million between 2019 and 2025.

How was the investment realized?+

In February 2026 the sale of control of Dan Transportation was signed with an investor group led by Dan's CEO, with Clal Insurance, Leumi Partners and Mizrahi-Tefahot Invest, at an effective valuation of about NIS 2.8 billion, as reported. The sale of Value LBH's holdings closed in June 2026, after Competition Authority approval.

How are Dan Transportation and Dan Real Estate related?+

Both were part of the Dan Group. Before the exit, the real estate business was separated into a distinct company, Dan Real Estate, which Value LBH retained after selling Dan Transportation. In August 2026 a non-binding MOU was signed for its merger with Dunietz Elad; as of late October 2026 the transaction had not been completed.
