Mankai & Hinoman: From Investment Opportunity to Business Recovery and Growth

In 2022 an investor group led by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, together with Kibbutz Be'eri, acquired the business of food-tech company Hinoman, which grows the Mankai plant in the kibbutz greenhouses, after the company entered insolvency proceedings. Following the October 7, 2023 attack, operations at Be'eri were restored and the company is developing international markets.

The company and the product

Hinoman is an Israeli food-tech company that grows and develops products based on Mankai. Mankai is a tiny aquatic plant, about the size of a grain of couscous, regarded as one of the smallest plants in the world and used as a food ingredient. It is grown in greenhouses at Kibbutz Be'eri covering about 70 dunams (17 acres).

As reported, the plant is regarded as a superfood for its protein, iron, fiber and vitamin B12 content. This describes its nutritional composition and is not a health or medical claim.

From Investment Opportunity to Business Recovery and Growth

  1. 01

    Identifying the opportunity

    In January 2022 Hinoman entered a debt arrangement after collapsing under about NIS 170 million in debt. LBH's founders saw the business and technological potential of the operation despite its financial difficulties.

  2. 02

    Acquisition and recovery

    In 2022 the business was acquired in a court-supervised process for about NIS 6 million. Ownership: an investor group led by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski (74%) and Kibbutz Be'eri (26%).

  3. 03

    Through the October 2023 crisis

    On October 7, 2023, some of the greenhouses at Be'eri were damaged. Two days later employees returned to tend the crops, and operations at Be'eri were restored.

  4. 04

    New markets and growth engines

    Incorporating the plant into a range of food products, pursuing regulatory approvals to market in Europe, and developing markets in Japan, the United States and Europe. A future IPO is one option under consideration, not a certainty.

Questions and answers: Mankai

What is Mankai?+

Mankai is a tiny aquatic plant, about the size of a grain of couscous, regarded as one of the smallest plants in the world and used as a food ingredient. In Israel it is grown by Hinoman in greenhouses at Kibbutz Be'eri. As reported, it is regarded as a superfood for its protein, iron, fiber and vitamin B12 content.

What is Hinoman?+

Hinoman is an Israeli food-tech company that grows and develops Mankai-based products. After financial difficulties it entered a debt arrangement in January 2022, and in 2022 its business was acquired in a court-supervised process. It is now developing products and expanding into international markets.

Who acquired the Hinoman business?+

An investor group led by Shimon Ben Hamo and Ofer Linchevski, the founders of LBH, together with Kibbutz Be'eri. The business was acquired in 2022 for about NIS 6 million. Ownership is 74% for the investor group and 26% for Kibbutz Be'eri.

How is Hinoman connected to Kibbutz Be'eri?+

Hinoman's greenhouses are at Kibbutz Be'eri, and the kibbutz holds 26% of the company. After the October 7, 2023 attack on the kibbutz, employees returned to the greenhouses two days later and operations at Be'eri were restored.
